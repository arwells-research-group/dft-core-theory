# Reciprocal System Primer

## Purpose of This Primer

This file provides the ontological and historical orientation for the broader research program indexed in this repository.

It is intended for readers who want to understand:

- the **Reciprocal System lineage** of the work,
- why the program begins from **motion-first postulates** rather than from state-space or force-law formalism,
- why the current research places special emphasis on the **compact-fiber / local admissibility carrier** line,
- and how later layers such as **Σ-Order / Dual-Frame** and **Σ₂** fit into the larger program.

This document is an orientation note, not a full derivation.

---

## 1. The Reciprocal System as a Deductive Research Program

The starting point of this research program is the motion-first lineage associated with Dewey B. Larson’s **Reciprocal System of Theory**.

The key idea is not merely that motion is important, but that a physical theory can in principle be developed **deductively** from a very small number of general premises rather than by introducing separate special assumptions for each physical domain.

In this spirit, the Reciprocal System is best understood as a **deductive research program** with the following ambition:

- begin from a minimal postulational base,
- derive the existence and structure of the physical universe as necessary consequences,
- and avoid importing independent field-specific mechanisms unless forced by deduction.

This is a much stronger methodological commitment than ordinary effective-model construction.

The intended mode of development is therefore not:
- parameter fitting,
- domain-by-domain patching,
- or phenomenological aggregation,

but rather:
- **stepwise consequence tracing**,
- where the theory proceeds by a chain of “if this, then that” deductions from the starting premises.

That deductive posture is central to the present program as well.

---

## 2. Core Motion-First Commitments

The Reciprocal System lineage begins from the idea that:

- the universe is composed entirely of **motion**,
- motion exists in **three dimensions** and in **discrete units**,
- and motion is the relation between two reciprocal quantities: **space** and **time**.

On this view:

- space and time are not independent containers,
- motion is not secondary to a pre-given background,
- and physical structure must arise from the consequences of motion itself.

This motion-first stance is the deepest conceptual origin of the work collected in this repository.

---

## 3. What the Original Program Achieved — and What Was Historically Lacking

The Reciprocal System already provided a powerful conceptual and deductive framework.

It offered:

- a motion-first ontology,
- a reciprocal relation between space and time,
- a deductive attitude toward physical theory,
- and a broad claim that many features of the physical universe can be derived as consequences of the postulates.

However, in modern terms, the original program did not fully supply an explicit formal apparatus for:

- **local admissibility structure**,
- **compact carrier organization**,
- **projection architecture** between primitive motion and observable structure,
- or a modern mathematical language for expressing scalar-motion closure and readout structure with sufficient local precision.

This is not a criticism of the original insight.
It is the main reason further reconstruction work is needed.

The present program should therefore be understood as:

- preserving Larson’s motion-first starting point,
- while developing a more explicit and modern formal expression of consequences that the original postulates already motivate.

## 4. Why the Compact-Fiber Reconstruction Matters

The current deepest accomplishment of the research program is the recovery of a **compact local admissibility carrier**.

In the present work, the compact-fiber line is important because it provides:

- a mathematically explicit local carrier for admissible motion structure,
- a way to express closure, sector structure, and readout classes more rigorously,
- and a bridge between motion-first postulates and concrete derived physical structure.

In this sense, the compact-fiber line is not merely another application or notation.
It is the current strongest candidate for a **faithful mathematical realization** of consequences latent in the motion-first starting point.

This is why the compact-fiber work now occupies a special place in the repository:

- it is the deepest current reconstruction line,
- and many later projection-level or application-level results should be understood relative to it.

## 5. Layer Structure of the Present Research Program

The repository organizes a single developmental program in several layers.

### 5.1 Origin Layer — Reciprocal System Lineage

This is the deepest origin layer.

It contains:

- the motion-first premise,
- reciprocal space/time,
- and the general deductive posture of the program.

### 5.2 Carrier Reconstruction Layer — Compact Fiber

This is currently the deepest reconstruction layer of the program.

It seeks to recover a compact local admissibility structure capable of expressing:

- closure,
- local sector organization,
- readout classes,
- and downstream physical consequences.

This is presently the central accomplishment of the program.

### 5.3 Projection Architecture Layer — Σ-Order / Dual-Frame

Σ-Order theory (ΣO), historically descended from Dual-Frame Theory, is the **reduced projection architecture** of the broader program.

It preserves structural residue beyond standard QM and makes certain admissibility boundaries explicit, but relative to the deeper carrier level it is **degenerate**.

Its role is still important:

- it provides a clear T/S projection architecture,
- it tracks winding sectors, coherence, and projection-closure stability,
- and it offers a productive reduced layer for many domain analyses.

But it should not be confused with the deepest ontological trunk of the program.

### 5.4 Methodology Layer — Σ₂

The **Σ₂ framework** is a methodological layer.

It is a framework for **projection-closure admissibility** and **representational refusal**.
It does not define the deepest ontology or generate physical dynamics.
Its purpose is to classify when reduced or symmetric representations fail under:

- continuation,
- intervention,
- or re-projection,

relative to richer carrier structure.

### 5.5 Empirical and Domain Layers

Above the previous layers sit:

- domain modules,
- empirical test repositories,
- and application-facing papers.

These show how the deeper carrier and projection layers manifest in measurable physical or informational domains.

## 7. What This Primer Does Not Claim

This primer does not claim that:

- the present program has wholly superseded Larson,
- the compact-fiber reconstruction is the final completed form of the theory,
- or all later branches are equally foundational.

Instead, it makes a narrower and more accurate claim:

- the Reciprocal System provides the motion-first starting point,
- the compact-fiber line is currently the deepest and strongest reconstruction layer,
- Σ-Order / Dual-Frame is the reduced projection architecture,
- Σ₂ is the methodological admissibility layer,
- and the empirical repositories are downstream manifestations of this larger program.

That is the intended orientation for the repository as a whole.

This research program should be read as a layered development in the **Reciprocal System lineage**.

Its present structure is:

1. **Motion-first origin** in Larson’s postulates,
2. **Compact-fiber reconstruction** as the deepest current carrier-level accomplishment,
3. **Σ-Order / Dual-Frame** as the reduced projection architecture,
4. **Σ₂** as the projection-closure admissibility methodology,
5. **domain and empirical repositories** as measurable downstream expressions.

Readers who keep these layers distinct will find the repository much easier to navigate and interpret correctly.
